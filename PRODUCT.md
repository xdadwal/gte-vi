# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Anonymous people in Delhi NCT browsing nearby community reports outdoors on phones, and a small trusted operator team managing incidents and host-confirmed refuges on desktops or phones.

## Product Purpose
Grand Theft Elections is a parody-themed community map. Publish fresh anonymous danger ratings, notify nearby foreground users, and calculate walking routes to curated refuges while avoiding reported areas. Reports and absence of reports never verify safety.

## Brand Personality
Urban, direct, playful. Original GTA-inspired typography and star motifs; calm, literal operational copy.

## Anti-references
No proprietary GTA assets, neon-on-white text, translucent controls, decorative dashboard cards, forced dialogs, or crowded mobile panels. No background tracking or public participant layer.

## Design Principles
Map first. Fast reporting. Preserve user control over rerouting. Explain freshness and availability. Keep operator work familiar and traceable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
English initially. WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, 44px touch targets, reduced motion, explicit labels alongside colours, daylight-readable surfaces.
